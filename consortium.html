Consortium

Seven partners, five countries

INTEGRA brings together healthcare providers, research institutes, universities and public health authorities from Southern, Northern, Eastern and Western Europe, with complementary expertise in neuromotor rehabilitation, telerehabilitation and health service modelling.

Coordinator

IRCCS Fondazione Don Carlo Gnocchi (FDG)

Florence, Italy

PROMISE@LAB – dongnocchi.it

Fondazione Don Carlo Gnocchi is an Italian non-profit foundation that has been caring for people with disabilities, older people and patients in need of rehabilitation for more than 70 years, through a network of centres in nine Italian regions. Its Florence centre is recognised by the Italian Ministry of Health as a Scientific Institute for Research, Hospitalisation and Healthcare (IRCCS) in rehabilitation, and hosts research laboratories including PROMISE@LAB and the Complex Neuromotor Rehabilitation Unit, run together with the University of Florence.

Role in INTEGRA

FDG coordinates the project and leads project management (WP1). It developed and certified the MAIA telerehabilitation platform used in the Italian, Spanish and Romanian pilots. It leads the definition of the system requirements, the pilot study protocol and ethical approval, and the impact assessment framework, and runs the Italian urban pilot site.

Partner

Azienda USL Toscana Centro (ASL)

Florence, Italy

uslcentro.toscana.it

Azienda USL Toscana Centro is the public local health authority of central Tuscany, serving the areas of Florence, Prato, Pistoia and Empoli. As part of the Italian National Health Service, it provides hospital, primary and community care, including rehabilitation, to the people living in its territory.

Role in INTEGRA

As the public health and care provider of the consortium, ASL brings the perspective of the health system and of policymakers. It leads the work on exploitation, policy recommendations and dissemination (WP5), including the assessment of policy and regulatory impact, coordinates the analysis of the pilot data, and runs the Italian rural pilot site.

NL

Partner

Roessingh Center of Rehabilitation (RCR)

Enschede, The Netherlands

roessingh.nl

Roessingh, in Enschede, provides medical specialist rehabilitation for children and adults. It is the only provider of the full range of medical specialist rehabilitation in the east of the Netherlands, and patients from across the country come to it for its specialised expertise. Its research and innovation department, Roessingh Research and Development (RRD), is a scientific research centre for rehabilitation technology and eHealth that has studied remote rehabilitation for more than 15 years. This work gave rise to Telerevalidatie, an online portal and app for rehabilitation that is widely used in Dutch rehabilitation care.

Role in INTEGRA

RCR leads the analysis and co-creation of the local ecosystems (WP2), guiding the involvement of patients, caregivers and professionals, and acts as technological manager of the consortium. It also leads the pre-validation of the local INTEGRA solutions and the Social Return on Investment strategy, and runs the Dutch pilot, building on the Telerevalidatie platform.

NO

Partner

Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU)

Trondheim, Norway

ntnu.edu

The Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) is Norway's largest university, with its main campus in Trondheim and a strong international profile in science, technology and health. Its health technology research brings together engineering, medicine and the social sciences to develop digital solutions for health and care, including telemedicine and welfare technology.

Role in INTEGRA

NTNU leads the development of the INTEGRA telerehabilitation framework (WP3), including the cross-country data interoperability framework and the central repository of best practices.

Instituto de Salud Carlos III (ISCIII)

Madrid, Spain

isciii.es

The mission of the Instituto de Salud Carlos III (ISCIII) is to contribute to improving the health of all citizens and to combat disease by fostering research and innovation in health sciences and biomedicine, and by providing specialized scientific-technical services and educational programs for the National Health System.

The Telemedicine and Digital Health Research Unit at the ISCIII has the mission of promoting and developing research, development, training, and innovation projects in the field of information and communication technologies applied to the health sector.

Role in INTEGRA

ISCIII leads the design and development of the INTEGRA system architecture and provides technical support during the pilots. It also contributes to the evidence that will inform policy and practice across the EU.

Partner

Gerencia Regional de Salud de Castilla y León (GRS)

Valladolid, Spain

saludcastillayleon.es

Role in INTEGRA

The Regional Health Service of Castile and León (GRS) participates in INTEGRA as a clinical partner through the Valladolid West Primary Care Directorate and leads Work Package 4 (WP4), which focuses on evaluation and validation. The team helps coordinate the regional pilot and brings a primary care perspective to the design and evaluation of a home-based telerehabilitation programme for people living with chronic low back pain, Parkinson’s disease or the long-term effects of a stroke. The pilot will assess how the programme can be integrated into routine clinical practice, taking into account the needs of patients and healthcare professionals.

RO

Collaborator

Carol Davila University of Medicine and Pharmacy (UMFCD)

Bucharest, Romania

umfcd.ro

Carol Davila University of Medicine and Pharmacy (UMFCD) in Bucharest is one of the oldest and largest medical universities in Romania, with roots in the national school of medicine founded by Carol Davila in the 19th century. It trains doctors, dentists, pharmacists and other health professionals, and carries out clinical research in university hospitals. Its Discipline of Physical and Rehabilitation Medicine, based at the Elias University Emergency Hospital, has long experience in neurological rehabilitation and in advanced rehabilitation and assistive technologies.

Role in INTEGRA

UMFCD takes part as a collaborator with its own funding. It leads the exploitation, sustainability and dissemination strategy, so that INTEGRA results reach health systems beyond the pilot sites, and runs the Romanian pilot.
