Accessibility

Accessibility statement

INTEGRA works for people with disabilities, so we want this website to be usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technologies.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Text and interface colours meet the WCAG contrast requirements.
  • The website can be used with a keyboard only, with a visible focus indicator and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Pages use headings, landmarks and lists so that screen readers can navigate them.
  • Images that carry information have text alternatives; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets and computers, and text can be enlarged up to 200% without loss of content.
  • Animations are kept to a minimum and respect the "reduce motion" setting of your device.

Known limitations

  • Some partner logos are provided by the partners as images and may not be fully sharp at high zoom levels.
  • The website is available in English, Italian, Romanian, Norwegian, Dutch and Spanish. Translations are being reviewed by native speakers, and news items and the EU funding statement are published in English only.
  • Documents published in the future (for example reports or PDFs) may not always be fully accessible. If you need a document in a different format, please contact us.

Feedback

If you find a barrier on this website, or need information in a different format, please write to us. We will do our best to help.
