Privacy notice

This website presents the INTEGRA project. It does not ask you for any personal data, does not use cookies and does not track you.

Who is responsible for this website

The INTEGRA website is managed by the project coordinator, Fondazione Don Carlo Gnocchi ETS (IRCCS Fondazione Don Carlo Gnocchi), registered office: Via Carlo Girola 30, Milan, Italy, which acts as data controller for the limited processing described below.

What data we collect

  • No forms, accounts or newsletters. You can browse the whole website without giving us any personal information.
  • No cookies and no tracking. The website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or social media plug-ins. Fonts and images are served directly from this website.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. To deliver the pages and keep the service secure, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address. For details, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Emails. If you write to one of the contact addresses on this website, we use your email address and the content of your message only to reply to you, and we do not share them with third parties.

People shown on this website

The names, roles and photos of the members of the INTEGRA team are published with their consent, to present the people working on the project. Anyone shown on the website can ask for their information to be changed or removed at any time by writing to the contacts below.

Links to other websites

This website links to the websites of the project partners, of the European Union and of THCS, and to the INTEGRA social media pages. When you follow these links, the privacy policies of those websites apply.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. In Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
