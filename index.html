Horizon Europe · THCS Partnership · 2026 – 2029

Telerehabilitation, closer to home

INTEGRA brings together seven partners from five European countries to co-create a telerehabilitation ecosystem that makes specialised rehabilitation accessible at home for people with disabilities living in the community, and for those who care for them.

Discover the project Follow our progress

INTEGRA stands for Co-creation of an INnovative TR Ecosystem accessible on a GRAnd scale to persons with disabilities living in the community

The challenge

Why telerehabilitation?

The need for rehabilitation is growing faster than health systems can respond, driven by population ageing and chronic conditions. Access is unequal, especially in rural and underserved areas.

Telerehabilitation, the remote delivery of rehabilitation through digital platforms, video calls and mobile apps, can bring therapy into people's homes and support continuity of care. Yet across Europe its uptake is still held back by infrastructural, regulatory, technological and socio-cultural barriers.

Our aims

What INTEGRA will do

  1. Co-create local ecosystems

    With patients, caregivers, professionals and decision-makers, INTEGRA maps the patient journey in each local context and co-designs telerehabilitation services that fit it.

  2. Improve health and quality of life

    Personalised rehabilitation at home, aiming to improve physical and cognitive functioning, participation and quality of life, for patients and their caregivers.

  3. Share what works

    A European knowledge base benchmarks telerehabilitation services and promotes the exchange of best practices between countries.

  4. Inform policy

    Original evidence, including a Social Return on Investment analysis, to guide policies, regulation and service models across the EU.

For people, by people

Who INTEGRA is for

  • People with disabilities

    Living in the community with chronic low back pain, after a stroke, or with Parkinson's disease.

  • Caregivers

    Family members and professional carers who support people through rehabilitation at home.

  • Health and care professionals

    Clinicians and therapists who want to deliver rehabilitation remotely, safely and effectively.

  • Policymakers

    Decision-makers shaping how telerehabilitation is regulated, reimbursed and scaled up.

Across Europe

One ecosystem, adapted to each country

INTEGRA will be piloted in four countries, adapting to the way care is organised in each, with 160 people with disabilities (80 with chronic low back pain, 40 after stroke, 40 with Parkinson's disease), 40 professional and 40 family caregivers.

  • IT

    Italy

    Integrated into the regional public telemedicine service in Tuscany, using the MAIA telerehabilitation platform.

  • NL

    The Netherlands

    INTEGRA solutions complement the existing Telerevalidatie platform.

  • ES

    Spain

    Telerehabilitation co-created as a new stand-alone service.

  • RO

    Romania

    Telerehabilitation co-created as a new stand-alone service.

  • NO

    Norway

    Leads the INTEGRA telerehabilitation framework and cross-country data interoperability.
